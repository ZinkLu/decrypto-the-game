## Design Context

### Users
Chinese- and English-speaking Decrypto players creating rooms and playing with friends or AI. Desktop-first with usable mobile layouts is the working assumption, pending the user's platform preference.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, mysterious, cinematic. The user explicitly wants a cool GUI inspired by the original board game's elements, preferably using Three.js.

### Aesthetic Direction
Original modeled black-and-ivory decoder machines, four numbered red-filter windows, code cards, interception tokens, and ruled notes. A warmly lit physical tabletop with strong red accents and expressive typography. Reference: https://www.scorpionmasque.com/en/decrypto .

### Design Principles
- Make the decoder machine the central visual and recurring interaction motif.
- Use real Three.js geometry, lighting, shadows and restrained parallax.
- Keep Chinese clues, word mappings and history legible in HTML.
- Cover every multiplayer role and phase, including disconnected and error states.
- Support small screens, keyboard use, reduced motion and WebGL fallback.

### Device lettering and mechanics (2026-09-20)
- Fixed chassis legends stay in English and never change with game state: NETWORK, ACTION, ORIGINAL GAME, the scoreboard, and hardware controls.
- All active displays, replaceable roster cards, phase indicators and printed receipts support Chinese and English. Preserve player names and submitted clues; use the word bank's supplied English keyword where available.
- Oscilloscope knobs vary continuously with physical end stops, and every knob visibly reshapes the figure. The tube is simulated from first principles (two oscillators, one beam, a decaying phosphor), realistic but playful rather than rigorous. Default to a stable locked 2:1 sine in Y/T mode. Controls are FREQ (signal-to-sweep ratio, locking at whole-number ratios), WAVE (blends steplessly through sawtooth, triangle, sine, square and pulse, marked with engraved glyphs), TIME/DIV (line down to a visible moving spot) and X-Y (rolls the sine steplessly into its Lissajous figure); the LOCK lamp beats or steadies with the oscillators. Every function has a physical control: the CRT itself is never a button. Keep a sharp beam and reduced-motion support.
- Cables need visible, labeled terminations and must clear controls and displays.
- Both languages use the same base type sizes; fit measured content and wrap long keywords without truncation.
- The floppy drive is manually loaded. The disk lies flat in its guides and moves perpendicular to the front panel, without an inclined insertion path. Show a central curved finger recess, a full first push until flush, a pause to regrip in the notch, a final recessed seating push, latch resistance and a separate eject-button/spring sequence.

### Central CRT (2026-09-22)
- The central display presents one task at a time with three underlined clue rows. The surrounding hardware carries room, team and score information.
- The top-left ORIGINAL GAME key opens the original board-game introduction, publisher, BGG and purchase links. The screen’s Guide button opens a single illustrated explanation, with localized text and theme-aware accents.
